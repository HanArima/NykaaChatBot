## Overview

Ever had that “I don’t know which lipstick shade to wear” moment before going out?
That’s exactly how this project started.
One day, while getting ready with my friends, all boys, and I couldn't ask them for advice. Therefore, I turned to Gemini AI and described my outfit (floral white top, trousers, heels, and dusky skin tone) and it helped me pick the perfect lipstick shade.
That’s when I thought:


*“Why can’t Nykaa users have an in-app AI stylist that instantly suggests the right shades and accessories for their outfit?”*


This project brings that idea to life.
**An AI-powered fashion assistant that chats with users, understands their outfit, skin tone, and vibe — and recommends matching products, shades, and styles**
